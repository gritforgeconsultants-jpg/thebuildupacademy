01 Mission

A trade is a foundation. We help people pour it.

Build-Up Academy is a construction-trades workforce training nonprofit. We open real pathways into construction for people who are ready to build a new life, including second-chance candidates, and we train them on a proprietary curriculum built from two decades in the trades.

Twenty years of construction judgment, broken down, practiced, and applied.

03 How the program works

Nine workbooks. Built floor by floor.

The curriculum is a proprietary set of nine workbooks. Trainees work through it the way a frame goes up: one level at a time, each resting on the last.

Module01

Plan Reading, Scope Takeoff & Quantity Math

Seven practical exercises. Real plans. Real math. Real-world preconstruction skills.

  1. Learn it

    Work through the workbooks in sequence, one module at a time.

  2. Apply it

    Practice on real plans and real math, held to a ±1% standard.

  3. Build your career

    Module completion supports placement evaluation.

Built for people ready to build something new.

Build-Up Academy is for people who want a real career in construction and are ready to earn it, including second-chance candidates. What matters is where you’re headed.

  • New entrantsStarting a construction career.
  • Career changersMoving into the industry from somewhere else.

05 For employers & contractors

A hiring pipeline built by someone who bids the work.

Contractors need people who arrive ready to learn and ready to work. Build-Up Academy trainees practice on real plans and real math, are held to a ±1% accuracy standard, and move up a ladder where module completion supports placement evaluation.

07 Founder

Arron Fain

Founder · 20-year carpenter turned construction estimator

Arron spent two decades as a carpenter before moving into construction estimating. He knows what the work asks of a person in the field, and what it takes to win it on paper. The BUA curriculum is that judgment, broken down, practiced, and applied.
