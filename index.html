01 Mission

A trade is a foundation. We help people pour it.

Build-Up Academy is a construction-trades workforce training nonprofit. We open real pathways into the trades for people who are ready to build a new life — including second-chance candidates — and we train them on a curriculum built by someone who has spent two decades on jobsites and in the estimating office.

A

Structured, not scattered

A proprietary nine-workbook curriculum gives every trainee a clear sequence to follow, from fundamentals forward.

B

Steel first, more trades next

We start with steel and structural work, then expand the program to other construction trades.

C

Doors open to second chances

Where someone starts doesn’t decide where they finish. We welcome people who are ready to put in the work.

02 How the program works

Nine workbooks. Built floor by floor.

Trainees work through the curriculum the way a steel frame goes up: one level at a time, each one resting on the last. Select a floor to explore a workbook.

  1. Learn

    Work through the nine-workbook curriculum in sequence.

  2. Build

    Develop trade skills, starting with steel and structural.

  3. Connect

    Meet contractors looking for people ready to work.

04 For employers & contractors

A hiring pipeline built by someone who bids the work.

Contractors need people who arrive ready to learn and ready to work. Build-Up Academy trains candidates on a curriculum written from both the field and the estimating desk, so they understand how the work gets built and why it matters to the job.

06 Founder

Arron Fain

Founder · 20-year carpenter turned construction estimator

Arron spent two decades as a carpenter before moving into construction estimating. He knows what the work asks of a person in the field, and what it takes to win it on paper.

$3M+

Revenue growth for a structural steel erector in under a year, from a system he built to bid commercial work in a trade he had never worked in.

$2.6M

Revenue growth for a second company from the first contract he secured for them.

Build-Up Academy turns that same systems thinking toward people: a clear, structured path into the trades.
